    Frequently asked questions

    Which VinFast models are available?

    We offer the VF 6, VF 7, VF MPV 7 and Limo Green. Visit the showroom or book a test drive to see them in person.

    What are the prices?

    Ex-showroom prices start from ₹18,19,000* for the VF 6 and ₹22,99,000* for the VF 7. The VF MPV 7 is at a festive price of ₹20,39,000*. Limo Green pricing is on request. Prices may change, so please confirm with the showroom.

    What is the driving range?

    As listed: VF 6 468 km, VF 7 532 km and VF MPV 7 517 km (ARAI). Real-world range varies with driving style, load and conditions.

    How long does fast charging take?

    The VF 7 charges from 10% to 70% in about 24 minutes*, depending on the charger and battery. Our team can advise on home charging for your model.

    Are there any offers running?

    Yes. The VF MPV 7 has a festive price, and free charging is offered till 31 March 2029* when you qualify under the current campaign. Terms apply; our team will confirm your eligibility.

    Can I get finance?

    Use our EMI calculator for an estimate. Actual loan terms are set by the lender, and our team will guide you on finance options.

    How do I book a test drive?

    Fill in the form on this page, message us on WhatsApp or call +91 92314 45060. Our team will confirm a convenient time.

    Can I exchange my old car?

    You can submit your current car’s details through the inquiry form. Our team will advise on exchange options.

    Do you provide service and spares?

    Yes. Patliputra VinFast covers sales, service and spares under one roof in Patna.

    What warranty do I get?

    Warranty terms depend on the model. Please ask our team for the vehicle and battery warranty details of the car you choose.

    Where is the showroom, and what are the timings?

    Plot No. 2421, NH 30, Bypass Road, Opposite Indian Oil Pump, Paijawa, Patna - 800009. Please call or WhatsApp us to confirm opening hours.

    What about delivery time, colours and insurance?

    These depend on the model and availability. Our team will confirm the details when you contact us.
